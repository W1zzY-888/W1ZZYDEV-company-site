# US privacy applicability checklist

Do not claim CCPA/CPRA or state-law compliance until this assessment is completed.

- Which US states are targeted or actually served?
- Annual revenue and data volume thresholds.
- Number of consumers/households/devices processed.
- Whether personal information is sold or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- Categories of personal information collected.
- Sensitive personal information collected or inferred.
- Purposes of processing.
- Service providers/processors and contracts.
- Retention periods by category.
- User rights workflow: access, deletion, correction, opt-out, appeal where applicable.
- Children/teen data handling.
- Cookie/analytics/ads stack.
- Security measures and incident process.
- Privacy notice update cadence.
- Contact channel for US privacy requests.
- Whether international transfers or non-US subprocessors affect notice language.
